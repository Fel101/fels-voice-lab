      Voice cloning

      Use a voice recording you may use to guide a local text-to-speech model. This creates performances from the clip; it does not train or fine-tune a model.

      1. Choose a recording

      WAV, FLAC, OGG, MP3 or M4A (M4A needs FFmpeg), up to 32 MB. Use a clean 6–30 second passage with one speaker. Files stay on this PC. Trim or clean longer recordings in Cast.

      2. Try the voice

      Review and edit in Cast

      Saving creates a named voice in Cast, not yet approved, and adds its recording to every Model studio recording picker. Listen before approving it in Cast; approval is never automatic. Test performances also appear in Perform lines.

      Finding permitted speech samples

      Public speech samples

      Mozilla Common Voice

      Multilingual speech research datasets. Review the chosen dataset license and current Mozilla Data Collective use terms; a dataset listing does not establish individual voice-cloning permission. Many clips are MP3, which Cast accepts as it is.

      Browse datasets · Read platform use terms

      Edinburgh CSTR VCTK

      English multi-speaker speech corpus with WAV recordings and transcripts. The dataset record specifies CC BY 4.0; retain attribution and note edits.

      Open the VCTK dataset record · CC BY 4.0 terms

      An audio license may not grant every voice-likeness, privacy, or publicity permission. For a recognizable person, prefer your own voice or a recording whose contributor explicitly permits voice cloning. Keep the source and license/permission details in the Cast origin and allowed-use fields.

      Import from Voice Slate

      Create or edit a character, its voices and its recordings

      Build your cast and approve a voice recording for each character. Your recordings and every saved version stay on this PC.

      Voice recording and its versions

      Original audio is saved unchanged. Each trim, channel choice, resample, or transcript edit creates a new immutable version. Breeze takes a voice recording of up to 60 seconds, and the Lab keeps every other recording to 60 seconds too.

      Choose a short noise-only part of the trimmed clip (no speech) as the noise profile. This spectral gate can reduce steady fan or hiss noise; it may color the voice and cannot separate overlapping speech or music. The original recording stays unchanged.

      A local draft to check word by word. It is not saved or marked exact. Speech recognition setup

      Perform lines

      Choose a character voice, direct a line, and audition variations. Every take is saved with the voice, voice recording and settings that made it.

      No characters yet. Add a character and voice in Cast, then come back to make takes.

      Details

      Filled in for you. Takes made for the same line name are listed together, and exports name the line by it.

      Model settings

      Controls shown here come from this checkpoint's supported performance schema. Each take keeps the values the model used, its defaults included.

                      Models & features
                      Each card is a voice model. Press Install on a model the app can install itself, and Test to check that an installed model speaks on this computer: the test loads it and speaks one short sentence. Models the app cannot install yet are listed under More models, with what each one needs.
                      

                      What these words mean
                      Each card's facts come from the Lab's records: its catalog, its adapters, the installers and the language screen (docs/LANGUAGE_MATRIX.md); where they say nothing, the card says "not stated". A card's Details say whether it is integrated and what the language screen heard. Integrated means the adapter is in this build. Installed means its environment and pinned checkpoint are on disk. Usable means a short test passed on this device. An integrated model does not mean every upstream feature has been exposed or tested. The RAM on a card is the free memory the Lab wants before it loads that model.

                      Production workflow
                      Design or clone in Model studio → save a character voice in Cast → direct and audition takes in Perform → render dialogue in Scenes → compare and rate.
                      Qwen CustomVoice preset voices and Phonon voices become Cast voices with no voice recording. Qwen VoiceDesign makes voice recordings for a voice that clones.
                      

                      Available outside the Lab's pages
                      Dataset preparation (tools/dataset_workbench.py) and word times for captions and lip-sync (align_takes.py) run from a terminal; the user guide describes both. Training is not enabled.
                      

                      Current limits
                      Only the Qwen engines stream audio while it is made; the others return the whole clip. Kokoro's Japanese and Mandarin voices need language packages its installer does not add. Automated signal checks do not establish voice quality: a person still has to listen.
