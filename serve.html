Voice cloning

Use a permitted reference recording to guide a local text-to-speech model. This creates performances from the clip; it does not train or fine-tune a model.

1. Choose a recording

WAV, FLAC, OGG, MP3 or M4A (M4A needs FFmpeg), up to 32 MB. Use a clean 6–30 second passage with one speaker. Files stay on this PC. Trim or clean longer recordings in Cast.

2. Try the voice

Review and edit in Cast

Saving creates a named candidate in Cast and adds it to every Model studio reference picker. Listen before approving it in Cast; approval is never automatic. Test performances also appear in Perform lines.

Finding permitted speech samples

Public speech samples

Mozilla Common Voice

Multilingual speech research datasets. Review the chosen dataset license and current Mozilla Data Collective use terms; a dataset listing does not establish individual voice-cloning permission. Many clips are MP3, while Cast currently accepts WAV, FLAC, and OGG.

Reference recording and versions

Original audio is saved unchanged. Each trim, channel choice, resample, or transcript edit creates a new immutable version. Breeze's native reference limit is 60 seconds; the local studio also caps other reference candidates at 60 seconds.

Choose a short noise-only part of the trimmed clip (no speech) as the noise profile. This spectral gate can reduce steady fan or hiss noise; it may color the voice and cannot separate overlapping speech or music. The original recording stays unchanged.

Models & features
Integrated means the adapter is in this build. Installed means its environment and pinned checkpoint are on disk. Usable means a short test passed on this device; the test loads the model and speaks one sentence. An integrated model does not mean every upstream feature has been exposed or tested. A card offers Install only for a model that has an installer; for the others it says what is missing. The RAM on a card is the free memory the Lab wants before it loads that model.

Production workflow
Design or clone in Model studio → save a character voice in Cast → direct and audition takes in Perform → render dialogue in Scenes → compare and rate.
Qwen CustomVoice preset speakers are reusable Cast identities and do not require a cloned reference. Qwen VoiceDesign creates references for a cloning identity.


Available outside this interface
Audio delivery packs (WAV, FLAC, OGG and captions) and dataset preparation currently use command-line tools. Training is not enabled. Chatterbox V3 and MOSS have isolated trial results but are not integrated here.


Remaining feature coverage
Breeze INT8 and native streaming, VoxCPM streaming, broader model integrations, word alignment and visemes, and local transcription are still pending. Only the Qwen engines offer native streaming here. Kokoro Japanese and Mandarin require additional language frontends. Listening scores still need human audition; automated signal checks do not establish voice quality.
